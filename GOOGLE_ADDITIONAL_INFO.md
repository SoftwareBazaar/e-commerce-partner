# ✅ Google Additional Info for Verification

## What to Put in "Additional info"

You can either:

### Option 1: Leave it Blank
Just click next/submit without filling it in. Google doesn't require this.

### Option 2: Add Brief Info (Recommended)

Copy and paste this:

```
NeuroAlgo is a financial trading platform offering:
- Free consultation bookings
- Mentorship programs
- Custom EA (Expert Advisor) development services
- Newsletter subscription

The app uses Google OAuth for user authentication only. 
Users can sign in with their Google account to access their bookings and dashboard.

Test user email: neuroalgoforexedge@gmail.com

The website is newly launched and uses Supabase for backend authentication.
```

---

## Then Click Submit

After filling in (or leaving blank), click the submit/next button to complete the verification request.

---

## What Happens Next

Google will review your request and either:
1. ✅ Approve it (most likely)
2. Ask for more information
3. Ask you to verify domain ownership

If they ask for domain verification, we can do that through your domain registrar.

---

## After Approval

Your Google Sign-In will be fully published and working!
